About

I am a computational mechanics and scientific AI researcher with a PhD from Imperial College London, working on finite element analysis, fracture mechanics, micromechanics, multiscale modelling and GPU-accelerated scientific computing. I develop scalable FEM and FFT solvers in JAX, MATLAB and C++.

At Laboratoire Navier I develop nonlinear homogenization and multiscale frameworks for polycrystalline and composite materials, turning heterogeneous microscale behaviour into efficient reduced-order constitutive models for large-scale structural simulation, as part of an ANR-funded collaborative programme with academic and industrial partners.

Research

Multiscale homogenization

Reduced-order and full-field models that carry microstructure up to the structural scale. Current work: a data-driven self-consistent clustering analysis (SCA) with a minimal offline stage, and a GPU-accelerated 3-D FFT-based homogenization framework in JAX that scales to 200 million degrees of freedom for full-field strain prediction in heterogeneous microstructures.

Earlier, at Imperial and as a visiting PhD student at Peking University: clustering-based reduced-order models for 3-D woven composites, long-fibre composites and polycrystals, with SCA and crystal-plasticity SCA up to two orders of magnitude faster than conventional numerical methods, and FFT and crystal-plasticity FFT solvers in MATLAB-MEX and OpenMP.

Computational fracture

The virtual element method for fracture: phase-field dynamic fracture and hydrogen-assisted cracking. With Yale University and the University of Pennsylvania: GPU-accelerated finite element solvers in JAX for dynamic, chemo-mechanical fracture and fatigue, up to 10× faster than CPU-only implementations, using matrix-free Krylov solvers, geometric multigrid and bound-constrained nonlinear optimization.

Scientific AI

Neural operators and generative models for full-field mechanics: Micrometer, a micromechanics transformer that predicts full-field responses of heterogeneous materials; FunDiff, diffusion models over function spaces for physics-informed generative modelling, to which I contributed numerical simulation and physical validation; and diffusion-based correction and super-resolution of simulation data.
